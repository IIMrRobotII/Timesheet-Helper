Privacy policy

Timesheet Helper · Last updated 2026-06-03

Timesheet Helper runs locally in Chrome. It copies your working hours from a Hilan timesheet into Malam payroll. It can also estimate monthly pay from that timesheet. It does not send data off your device.

Data stored locally

The extension stores the following in chrome.storage.local:

  • The hours you copy from Hilan, including per-day entry and exit times and vacation days.
  • Whether the extension is on, and whether the salary calculator is shown.
  • Your hourly rate, used only by the on-device salary calculator.
  • Your language and theme preferences.

The extension does not use chrome.storage.sync.

Page content

On supported Hilan pages, the content script reads your entry and exit times so it can copy them. On supported Malam pages it fills those times into the payroll form. The salary calculator reads the same timesheet in your browser to estimate pay.

The popup checks the active tab URL to decide whether to show Hilan or Malam controls. Full sync also checks supported open tab URLs so it can choose the Hilan and Malam tabs, and it may focus those tabs while copying and pasting. It does not store URLs.

The code queries only the time fields needed to copy and paste hours. It does not save, transmit, or sell credentials, names, ID numbers, payroll amounts, or other page content.

Network and third parties

The extension has no accounts, analytics, cookies, remote database, external API, or third-party service. It makes no network requests.

Permissions

  • storage saves the items listed above on your device.
  • Host access covers *.hilan.co.il and payroll.malam.com. Chrome grants host access by origin, even though the manifest syntax includes paths. The content script runs only on the Hilan attendance paths (https://*.hilan.co.il/Hilannetv2/Attendance/* and https://*.hilan.co.il/Hilannetv2/attendance/*) and the Malam payroll path (https://payroll.malam.com/Salprd5Root/faces/*).

Retention

Everything stays on your device until you remove it. Use Clear All Data in the popup to delete the copied hours and all settings, or uninstall the extension to remove its data from your browser.
